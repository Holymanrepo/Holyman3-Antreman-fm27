1. INSTALLATION OPTIONS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Option A (Recommended - Smart Setup Wizard):
  Run "FM27.Trainer.Setup.exe".
  - Choose your preferred language (English, Türkçe, Português) in the wizard.
  - Your Windows user profile's "Documents\Sports Interactive\Football Manager 27\graphics"
    folder is auto-detected. If your graphics pack is on another drive, click
    "Browse..." to select it.
  - Click "Install & Launch »" to install the app, create Desktop & Start Menu
    shortcuts, and register an official Windows Installed Apps (Add/Remove
    Programs) uninstaller entry.

• Option B (Portable / No-Install Executable):
  Simply double-click "FM27 Trainer.exe" to run immediately without installation.
  All 3 language dictionaries (English, Türkçe, Português) are embedded directly
  inside the executable.

2. KEY FEATURES IN v1.3.0
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Visual Pitch View & Real-Time Tactic Sync:
  - On the "Tactics" tab, a striped football pitch displays your Starting XI
    in their true pitch positions with player facepacks, role fit badges,
    IP/OOP role codes, and condition bars.
  - Switching between "Tactic 1 / Tactic 2 / Tactic 3" updates the active
    tactic in FM27 memory while preserving player pitch positions.

• Saved Starting XI Templates & "Apply Best XI to Game":
  - Click "Apply Best XI to Game" to automatically assign your highest-fit 11
    players into FM27's active tactic.
  - Type a template name (e.g. "Main XI", "Cup XI", "Rotation") and click
    "Save Current XI" to store up to 10 lineups and load them with one click.

• Interactive Training Session Swap (2-Click Swap & Right-Click Menu):
  - On "Training -> Upcoming Weeks", left-click any two training slots to swap
    them, or RIGHT-CLICK any slot to pick a replacement session from the full
    catalog and write it directly to FM27 memory.

• Unified FM27 Graphics Folder (Player Faces & Club Logos):
  - Automatically finds and loads both your connected club's crest in the
    sidebar and player portraits across the Squad and Tactics tabs from a
    single "graphics" folder.

• Squad Readiness & Tactical Understanding (100% Lock):
  - One-click top action bar on the Squad tab to Heal Everyone, Clear
    Suspensions, Max Everyone's Morale, and Maximise Tactical Understanding.
